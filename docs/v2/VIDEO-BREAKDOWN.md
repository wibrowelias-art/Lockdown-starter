## Look and setting

- Format: 720 x 1280 vertical, 30 fps, 122.4 s, stereo. First person through a very wide fisheye lens (room edges bend). Flat cel shading, thick black outlines, soft vignette.
- Title card at 0 s: "TUESDAY . 10:42 AM" and "Room 214 - Biology final".
- Subtitle colors by speaker: orange = PA, yellow = teacher, cyan = the red-sweater classmate ("Theo", our name, never named in the video), white = Noah (the player), pink = Zoe. Heavy rounded font, white or colored with a black outline.
- Room 214: ceiling light panels; a PA speaker beside the board that glows with ripple rings when it speaks; a green EXIT sign over a blue door with a tall narrow window and a lever handle; a round wall clock; a red-and-white LOCKDOWN poster ("1 LOCK, 2 LIGHTS, 3 OUT OF SIGHT") next to the door; window blinds on the left wall; a colorful bulletin board on the right wall; a bookshelf and gray lockers beside Noah's desk. The room is upstairs (teacher: "Outside. Not up here.").
- Board: "BIOLOGY FINAL 10:00 - 11:30", "NO PHONES. NO TALKING. Good luck!" and a drawn DNA helix.
- Noah's desk: answer sheet "BIOLOGY - FINAL EXAM, Name: Noah R." with bubbles and lines, a yellow pencil, a maroon pencil case, a silver water bottle. Gray sleeves, tan hands.
- People: teacher (mustard sweater, red-brown hair bun, round glasses, dark trousers; our name "Ms. Okafor"); classmate by the lockers (red sweater, black curly hair, huge white eyes); student in a pink-red sweater in front of Noah; green-top student behind her; yellow-top girl with a black hair bun; purple-top student; tall student in a blue sweater. Students seen from behind show no faces. Zoe's seat is empty; her purple backpack (white tag, yellow dot) sits under the desk beside a red one.
- Phone: dark chat screen, Zoe's messages gray, Noah's blue; new messages also pop up as cream banner cards with the time (10:46, 10:47, 10:52); red battery icon.
- Lockdown lighting: the room turns navy blue but stays readable. The door window and the strip under the door are pure bright white. Siren light alternates pink and blue through the blinds from 82 s.

## What v1 missed or got wrong

- Look: v1 was blocky and nearly black; the target is a clean outlined cartoon with readable navy lockdown scenes and a bright door strip.
- Missing lines: "This is a drill... right?" / "We don't run drills during finals", "Not up here", "Or... somebody has her phone".
- Missing details: feet shadows in the light strip, exact board text, "Noah R." on the sheet, speaker-colored subtitles, the red DON'T OPEN tag, Zoe's backpack under her empty desk, the red battery icon.
- Camera: v1 used a 70 degree lens; the video uses a far wider fisheye.
